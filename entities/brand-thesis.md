# Entity: brand thesis & voice

The throughline every project inherits when it faces outward. Owned by
[[homesteader-labs-site]] (the brand home + the voice files); referenced by all others.

## The thesis
**Off-grid / homestead self-reliance, framed as caloric & resource security.** Recurring
pillars seen across the site's content and strategy docs: *caloric security*, *decloudify* /
"fuck your inputs" (reducing dependence on cloud + external inputs), and field-documented,
tools-not-fluff practicality.

## The honest-fit AI angle
The model ([[model-registry]]) **refuses when it isn't sure** rather than guessing. The framing
that unifies the ML and the brand: a forager in the woods has no signal, so a small on-device
model isn't a compromise — it's the only thing that works. This "abstain over bluff" stance is
both a safety property (deadly-vetoes-safe) and a brand value. Keep them aligned. This "abstain
over bluff" instinct is the *outward* face of [[anti-slop-principle]] — the same stance expressed
as engineering across the constellation (hestia's determinism-over-intelligence, forager's refusal).

## Audience (2026-09-30)
**Makers are the audience, the garden is the subject.** The visitor the site wants has a 3D
printer, ESP32s and a yard, and could one day want the handheld ([[edge-hardware]]); gardeners
come along because everything built is about the garden. The property is the experiment: each
build log ships a measured number, a short clip and a printable part. Chosen over a field-hardware
platform, whose market would be lone-worker safety with its compliance and support load. Applies
to any project's outward writing about builds, [[hestia]]'s included.

## Voice
The site carries the canonical voice system: `voice.md`, `newsletter-voice.md`, `about-me.md`
(plus `GEMINI.md`). Any outward writing for *any* project — a forager_ml model card, a
field-station Space description, a hestia-facing blurb — should inherit from these, not reinvent
a tone. Don't copy them here; link down and defer.
